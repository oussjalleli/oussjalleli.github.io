research

My work brings together signal processing, computer vision, and multimodal AI to make sense of complex data. I develop methods for learning across modalities, capturing spatial and temporal patterns, and building robust, interpretable systems. Applications include visual understanding, audio-visual analysis, physiological sensing, and media forensics.
